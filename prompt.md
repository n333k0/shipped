You are a world-class brand strategist, conversion copywriter, product designer, UX architect, creative director, and web agency founder.

I am creating a PRODUCTIZED WEBSITE SERVICE.

This is NOT a design subscription like DesignJoy.

This is NOT an unlimited monthly design service.

This is NOT a traditional web agency.

The business sells websites as clearly defined products.

The core idea is:

BUY A WEBSITE LIKE YOU BUY A PRODUCT.

A client should be able to understand:

- exactly what they are buying
- exactly how much it costs
- exactly what is included
- approximately how long it takes
- exactly what the process looks like
- exactly when we can start
- what happens next

without sitting through a sales call, requesting a proposal, waiting for a quote, or entering a traditional agency process.

The service should feel closer to a modern SaaS company, software product, Linear, Stripe, DesignJoy, Framer, or a premium startup than a conventional agency.

I want you to create the COMPLETE WEBSITE STRATEGY, STRUCTURE, UX AND COPY for this company.

Do not give me an outline only.

Write the actual website.
