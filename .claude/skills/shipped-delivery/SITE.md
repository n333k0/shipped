# Reading the brief into a site

The client already told us what they want; the job is to read it precisely. Each brief field maps to decisions:

| Brief | Decides | How |
|---|---|---|
| Closest reference | Type scale, weight, radius, density, layout rhythm | Measured from `refs/<slug>.md`, adapted: their system, not their brand |
| Other references + traits | What else to borrow | Only the traits they ticked (typography, photography…) |
| Sliders | Mode, contrast, how much colour and type contrast | ≤40 leans left, ≥60 leans right, between is balanced |
| Creativity (`safe_experimental`) | How far the signature moment goes | Always one signature moment; above 60 it can be unusual |
| Motion | Amount of animation | still · subtle (fades, hover) · dynamic (scroll) · wild (interactive) |
| Type choice | Display/text pairing | Within their own brand fonts when they have them |
| Locked assets | What stays exactly as is | Logo, colours, typography, messaging |
| Goal | What every page ends on | The primary CTA, repeated |

## Typography

Use the client's own typefaces (from `source/` or `font_names`), or ones chosen from the references. Shipped's pairing (Inter Tight + Instrument Serif italic) belongs to Shipped; clients read it as ours.

## Content

- Real copy, real products, real prices, real photos from `source/`. Polish rough copy, keep every fact (prices, address, hours, payment terms).
- Numbers and claims come only from the client. Where a block needs proof they haven't given, write it so it stands without numbers, or leave a clear note.
- Product brands: the product photo leads. Let the photo's own background fill the hero and make the product large.

## Build and check

- Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by a `build-site.mjs` in the client folder; site JS wrapped in a module or IIFE.
- Check 1440px and a 390px frame (iframe in the real browser; headless Chrome can't go below ~500px), every page, horizontal overflow, the signature interaction, hover and touch states, reduced motion.
- Heroes: cap `min-height` (e.g. `min(100svh, 900px)`) so tall screens and full-page captures stay sane.
