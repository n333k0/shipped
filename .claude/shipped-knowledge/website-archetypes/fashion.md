# Fashion / Beauty

Category id `fashion` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Sell a look and a feeling, with products one tap away.
- **Typical structure:** Home (campaign, collections) · Collection · Product · Lookbook/Story · Stockists/Contact. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Shop / add to cart
- **Content to collect:** Campaign and product photography, collection names, sizing and shipping
- **Design language:** Editorial, image-led, bold typography; fashion brands can push further in v2/v3. Same selling rules as e-commerce
- **Technical needs:** Existing store platform, lookbook galleries
- **QA additions:** Product images keep their ratio; size and price visible on phones
