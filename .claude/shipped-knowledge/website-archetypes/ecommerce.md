# E-commerce / Product

Category id `ecommerce` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Sell: products, prices and the buy button calm and visible on every screen.
- **Typical structure:** Home (hero product, collections, story, proof) · Collection · Product · About/Showroom · FAQ/Shipping · Contact. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Add to cart / buy; secondary: visit the showroom
- **Content to collect:** Product photos (on light and dark), prices, payment terms, shipping and returns, product names and descriptions
- **Design language:** v1 is the safe store; product photography large; effects only where they make a product look better; experiments in v2/v3 never hide products or prices. Lighting/furniture brands count here too
- **Technical needs:** Existing checkout (Shopify, Tiendanube…) kept unless scoped otherwise; up to 25 products on the shop add-on
- **QA additions:** Prices and buy button visible at 390px; product images not stretched; cart reachable in one tap
