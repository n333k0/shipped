# Culture / Art / Media

Category id `culture` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Make the programme or the publication easy to explore.
- **Typical structure:** Home (current programme or issue) · Programme/Archive · Item template · Visit/About · Newsletter. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Visit, buy a ticket, subscribe or read
- **Content to collect:** Programme or articles, dates, images with credits
- **Design language:** Editorial grids, expressive type, the content as the design. Avoid corporate polish
- **Technical needs:** CMS for programme or articles, newsletter, ticketing link
- **QA additions:** Dates and times correct; archive navigable; credits present
