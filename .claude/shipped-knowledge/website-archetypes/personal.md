# Personal brand

Category id `personal` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Put a person and their work forward.
- **Typical structure:** Home (who, what, selected work or offer) · Work/Writing · About · Contact. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Contact, book, subscribe or buy their offer
- **Content to collect:** Portrait photography, bio, work samples, offers
- **Design language:** A voice before a template; type and layout carry the personality
- **Technical needs:** Newsletter, booking link, simple CMS for writing
- **QA additions:** Contact path obvious; portrait and work images sharp
