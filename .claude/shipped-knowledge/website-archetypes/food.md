# Food / Beverage

Category id `food` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Make it look delicious and make the next step obvious (buy, find, book).
- **Typical structure:** Home (product or dish, story, where to buy or book) · Products/Menu · Story · Stockists/Visit · Contact. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Goal mapping:** `book_call` ("Get calls and bookings") means a table or room reservation here.
- **Primary conversion:** Buy, find a stockist or book a table
- **Content to collect:** Food photography, product range, ingredients as supplied, stockists or address
- **Design language:** Appetite first: large food photography, warm colour, personality in type; restaurants share the hospitality rules
- **Technical needs:** Shop or delivery links, maps, reservation link
- **QA additions:** Images load fast on mobile; menu readable at 390px
