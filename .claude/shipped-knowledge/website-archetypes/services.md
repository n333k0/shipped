# Professional services

Category id `services` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Make an expert credible and easy to contact.
- **Typical structure:** Home (what they solve, for whom, method, proof, CTA) · Services · About/Team · Insights (optional) · Contact/Booking. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Book a call or send an enquiry
- **Content to collect:** Services with outcomes in their words, credentials only as supplied, team photos, FAQ
- **Design language:** Calm, editorial, trustworthy; strong typography over stock imagery. Avoid handshake stock photos and buzzwords
- **Technical needs:** Booking link (Cal.com/Calendly), contact form, CRM if requested
- **QA additions:** Form validation and confirmation; booking link works; phone tap-to-call
