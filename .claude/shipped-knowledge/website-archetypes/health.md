# Health / Wellness

Category id `health` (brief field `project.industry`). Starting profile: refine it with each project of this category through [project-learnings](../project-learnings.md).

- **Job of the site:** Build trust and make the first appointment or purchase easy.
- **Typical structure:** Home (promise, approach, services or products, practitioners, proof) · Services/Treatments · About · Book/Contact. Trim to the purchased package.
- **Primary conversion:** Book an appointment or buy
- **Content to collect:** Services, practitioners and credentials as supplied, prices if public, regulatory wording from the client
- **Design language:** Calm, warm, human photography; clarity over decoration. Never invent medical claims
- **Technical needs:** Booking integration, forms with privacy care
- **QA additions:** No medical or performance claim without a client source; forms state how data is used
